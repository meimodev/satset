# SatSet domain terms

Selected terms settled in product design. The wider venue vocabulary lives in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md).

## Staff navigation

**Staff destination**:
A work area in the main navigation that a staff account is allowed to open. A venue may also hide a destination when that kind of work is switched off.
_Avoid_: Menu (also names the food catalogue)

## Visit spending

**Tagihan layanan (service bill value)**:
The sum of sent, non-voided order item prices for one visit, across every order batch and before tax, discounts, or payments. It is the limit for a visit expense.
_Avoid_: Unpaid balance, final bill total

**Pengeluaran layanan (service expense)**:
Cash spent on guests during a visit and recorded against that visit. It does not reduce what the guests owe.
_Avoid_: Petty cash, discount

## Ordering and stock

**Menu item**:
A sellable choice that may have a recipe consuming ingredients. It has no separate stock balance; an item sold by the unit uses a one-unit ingredient and a one-to-one recipe.
_Avoid_: Ingredient, stock item

**Available portions**:
The additional portions of one menu configuration that current ingredient stock can cover after other lines in the cart are considered. The number is provisional until the order is sent and does not reserve stock.
_Avoid_: Menu stock, reserved portions
