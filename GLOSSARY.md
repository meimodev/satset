# SatSet venue language

Terms for staff navigation and visit spending. The wider venue vocabulary lives in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md).

## Language

**Staff destination**:
A work area in the main navigation that a staff account is allowed to open. A venue may also hide a destination when that kind of work is switched off.
_Avoid_: Menu (also names the food catalogue)

## Visit spending

**Tagihan layanan (service bill value)**:
The sum of sent, non-voided order item prices for one visit, across every order batch and before tax, discounts, or payments. It is the limit for a visit expense.
_Avoid_: Unpaid balance, final bill total

**Pengeluaran layanan (service expense)**:
Cash spent on guests during a visit and recorded against that visit. It does not reduce what the guests owe.
_Avoid_: Petty cash, discount
