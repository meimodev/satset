Reads six independent signals — status, money, service state, ownership, bookings and staleness — and lays them out so the worst one is what a waiter sees first. Everything below the pax row is conditional, so a quiet table stays a number and a status and nothing else. Table number: mono 26 (phone) / 36 (tablet), scaled down to fit rather than ellipsised. Radius 22 phone / 20 tablet. Ticks once per second. Ownership: "Punya saya" as an accent chip on your own tables, the other waiter's initials as a neutral nameplate otherwise. Your own tables also take an accentBorder under lembut. The stale banner runs edge to edge across the foot and names the overrun and the action — never just a colour. Its foreground is picked by luminance: white on `warn` amber is ~2:1 and fails AA, and this is the one thing on the card that must be readable at a glance. All text nodes are merged into one semantics node — without it TalkBack reads a card as four unrelated stops. Merged, one swipe announces "Meja 4, terisi, Punya saya" in visual order.

Brutal paints the semantic *soft* token as a full slab — its softs are near-saturation blocks, where lembut's are 14% tints meant to sit behind a border. Status text stays ink on the slab: the fill already carries the signal, and green-on-green fails contrast. Crit cards sit on a doubled shadow — a red slab behind the ink one — so a screen of black-edged cards still has one that reads as lifted further. Pressing translates the card 3px into its own shadow instead of scaling.
