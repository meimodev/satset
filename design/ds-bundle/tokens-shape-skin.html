Kirim ke dapurBatal
Every radius collapses to 0. Every rule snaps to 3px solid ink — except a width:0 call site, which is a border switched *off*, not a thin one. SatBox.d() lifts a card onto a hard (3,3) un-blurred ink shadow, but only when it paints its own fill, has a border and a radius, and is rectangular: a borrowed-surface decoration would render as a solid ink slab with the content nowhere in sight. Wrong beats invisible. SatShape.caps() uppercases display and label copy — Indonesian has no casing rules this breaks.
