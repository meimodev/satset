AdminPage strip

Every admin route opens with the same embedded strip — sans 20/w600 title over a mono 11 uppercase subtitle, on a border0 underline. It is not an app bar: it scrolls with nothing and owns no back button, because admin routes live inside the shell and the tab bar is the way out. The subtitle takes an optional leading dot, used by Reports for live-vs-snapshot freshness.

Eight sections, each carrying a live badge that answers the question the section exists to answer — "24 meja · 3 zona", "Pajak 11% · Service 5%". The owner should be able to decide whether to tap without tapping. A section that needs attention swaps its badge to warn and borders the whole card warn. Alert is a card-level state, not a dot in a corner: at a glance across eight tiles, a 6px dot is invisible. Tints are per-section and drawn from the semantic set at 12% — the only place in the app where colour is used for identity rather than status, and it works because the label is right underneath it.

A report that includes today is Live and gets a success dot; anything else is a frozen Snapshot on a muted dot. The word is written out next to the dot — an owner exporting numbers to an accountant needs to know whether they will change under them.
