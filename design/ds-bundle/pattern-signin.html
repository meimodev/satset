Three trailing glyphs, three meanings, none of them colour-only: a filled tick (paired and selected), a broadcast icon (discovered but unpaired — tapping claims it), an empty ring (paired, not selected). The leading dot pulses (1600ms, reverse) so a live LAN reads as alive. Under reduced motion it holds at the midpoint rather than stopping dark. The empty state is a sentence, not a spinner: "Mencari server di jaringan… atau pasangkan manual lewat QR." — multicast is blocked on plenty of venue Wi-Fi, and the fallback has to be visible before the user gives up.

Once paired the list collapses to this one card and tapping it re-opens the PIN sheet. The pencil is the only way back to the list — changing server is rare and destructive to a shift, so it is deliberately one step off the happy path. Reachability is debounced: a single failed probe stays on the neutral "Memeriksa sambungan…" in warn, and only two consecutive failures escalate to urgent "Server tidak terjangkau". A Wi-Fi blip must not flash red.

Lupa password?
⚠ Perlu koneksi internet untuk verifikasi admin.
Admin is the only role that needs the internet — staff PINs are verified by the LAN server. Two cold-boot blocks get their own copy, and neither blames the user: "stale" explains that internet is needed and what to do; "ineligible" says access was revoked and who to ask. While a cached admin session is being restored the form is replaced by a loading screen rather than being shown and then redirected away — a sign-in form that flashes and vanishes reads as a failed login.
