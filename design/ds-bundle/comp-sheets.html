Six digits, submitted automatically on the sixth — there is no confirm button, because a waiter typing a PIN one-handed while carrying a tray should not need a second target. Failure is physical, not textual-only: the dot row shakes (sin, 4 cycles, damped, 360ms), the digits clear, and the helper line turns urgent mono. Under reduced motion the shake is skipped and the message alone carries it. The helper line has three states in one slot — blank when empty, "n / 6 digit" while typing, "Memverifikasi…" once complete — so the sheet never grows or shrinks mid-entry. The live reachability pill sits under the subtitle and is re-probed on open, because a stale heartbeat would let a waiter type six digits into a server that is already gone. Seeded credentials render in debug builds only.

The period is a read-only pill, not a picker. The export inherits whatever range is live on the Reports screen behind it, so there is no way to export a window the owner is not currently looking at. An unavailable kind greys to 0.4 and stays inert rather than disappearing, with a sentence naming the fix ("Laporan belum siap — buka laporan dulu…"). A control that vanishes teaches nothing. CSV is written UTF-8 with a BOM so Excel opens Indonesian text and Rupiah without mangling them — the export exists to be read by an accountant, not by this app.

This sheet lives on the Me screen, not under /settings. /settings is gated on editSettings; every waiter must be able to set the look of the handset in their own hand without an owner unlocking it (ADR-0045). Selection is stated by a radio glyph, not by the accent tint alone — the tint is unreadable against some of the swatches it sits next to. The swatch is three bands (ground, accent, ink) rather than a miniature screenshot. Enough to tell six palettes apart at a glance; not enough to pretend it previews a layout it cannot. The sheet is opaque on purpose: a translucent barrier would judge each swatch through a tint of the palette being replaced.
