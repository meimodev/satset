Labelled field — rest / focus / error

⚠ Email tidak valid.
The label is mono 10 / +1.2em uppercase, sitting above the field rather than floating inside it — a floating label vanishes the moment the field has content, and this form is filled once by someone who has never seen it before. Border0 at rest, accent on focus, urgent on error. The error border and the error line appear together; neither is used alone, because a red outline with no sentence tells a user something is wrong but not what. Errors clear on the next keystroke, not on the next submit.
