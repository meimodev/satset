Every group states its own obligation in the header — WAJIB / OPSIONAL / BEBAS PILIH / TIMING — so a waiter never has to tap the CTA to discover what's missing. When a required group is unanswered the CTA does not grey out silently: it swaps its label to "Pilih wajib" at 0.4 opacity, naming the blocker instead of just refusing. Shape carries the selection rule: radios are circles (r-pill), checkboxes are 6px squares. The tick is drawn in accentInk on a filled accent box — selection is never tint-only. Sold-out options come from ingredient stock (ADR-0040) and are per-option, not per-dish: "Extra pedas · habis" while the sate itself is still orderable. The note field caps at 80 chars and says where the text lands ("tampil ke dapur") — a waiter typing an allergy needs to know the kitchen will see it.
