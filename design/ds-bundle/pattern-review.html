Blocks render in a fixed order — drinks, starters, mains, sides, desserts, fire-now — regardless of the order items were tapped. The kitchen's timeline is the organising principle here, not the waiter's. Each header states the block's own firing rule in plain words: "auto-bakar" for drinks and fire-now, "ditahan sampai dibakar" for everything else. Held vs. fired is the state a waiter is most often wrong about, so it is written out per block rather than implied by the course colour.

A menu-first draft has no table yet, so the destination is chosen at commit time rather than at the start. Two tiles, no default, no "remember my choice" — picking wrong here seats a party at the wrong table.
