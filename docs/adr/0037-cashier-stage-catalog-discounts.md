# Cashier-stage catalog discounts (whole-order and per-line)

Venues need to knock money off a bill — a member rate, a Friday promo, a goodwill gesture on one dish. Two questions decided the shape of the feature: **who** may reduce a total, and **when**. This ADR puts discounts entirely on the [[Settlement (recording payment)|settlement]] side, owned by the [[Cashier]], stored against a **receipt**, and drawn from an **owner-defined catalog** rather than typed freehand. [ADR-0038](0038-discount-tax-stacking-order.md) covers the money math; [ADR-0039](0039-settled-total-over-redefining-net-total.md) covers the reporting column.

## Decision

- **Cashier-stage only.** A discount is applied at settlement, never during order-taking. The [[Waiter]] flow, the cart [[Estimasi (cart estimate)|Estimasi]], and the guest self-order SPA are untouched and always quote **undiscounted** totals. Money authority stays on the one screen that already has it (`settleBill`), instead of turning every waiter tablet into a pricing surface whose discounts would then have to survive reseat, [[Pindah meja (Move table)|pindah meja]], and line edits.
- **Receipt-scoped, one table, nullable target.** A single `Discounts` table carries `receiptId` plus a **nullable `ticketId`**: null ⇒ whole-order discount, set ⇒ line discount against the units *that receipt owns*. "Whole order" and "individual menu" are one feature with one write path and one audit trail, not two mechanisms. `ON DELETE CASCADE` on `receiptId`.
- **Catalog, not ad-hoc.** Cashiers **pick a preset**; they cannot type a percentage. A new `DiscountPresets` table holds `{id, name, scope('order'|'line'), kind('percent'|'fixed'), value, active, sortOrder}`, edited by the owner in a **"Diskon" section of Venue Settings** (`editSettings`). `scope` is what stops "Potongan 50rb" being applied to a 25k line.
- **Snapshot at apply.** A `Discounts` row copies `name`, `kind`, and `value` off the preset and keeps `presetId` only as a **weak reference for reporting**. Editing "Member 10%" to 15% next month does not rewrite last month's bills; deleting a preset does not corrupt history. Same pattern as the [[Modifier snapshot (on a sent line)]]. This is why presets are **hard-deleted** rather than archived, with an `active` flag for seasonal hiding.
- **No stacking.** Two partial unique indexes enforce **one order discount per receipt** and **one line discount per line**. A venue wanting "member + happy hour" makes one preset with the combined value — an owner-time decision made once where the arithmetic can be checked, rather than a cashier-time compounding question ("10% then 20%" is 28% or 30% depending on an implementation detail nobody at the till can see).
- **Authority: capability plus step-up.** The pre-existing but never-wired `applyDiscount` capability gates the UI. A cashier without it can still apply one by pulling a **manager step-up**, reusing the void/comp approval flow; the row records `byUserId` and a nullable `approvedByUserId`. An all-or-nothing role grant would force the venue to choose between unsupervised discounting every shift and a manager physically logging into the till for every promo.
- **Frozen at payment.** Discounts are freely added, removed, and swapped while a receipt is unpaid — nothing has happened yet, so the row is edited in place rather than compensated. Once paid, the receipt must go through the existing audited **reopen** to change. Editing a paid receipt's discount directly would create a "total went *up* after payment" state that `Payments.isRefund` cannot express (a refund is money returning to the guest, not money the guest now owes).
- **Line discounts are itemized-only.** An **even** receipt owns no lines, so a line discount has nothing to attach to; even mode explicitly abandons tracking who ordered what, which makes per-item pricing inside it self-contradictory. `split-even` already deletes and rebuilds every receipt for the visit, so the cascade disposes of line discounts for free — the only addition is a warning in the confirm dialog.
- **Split-bill distribution.** A **percent** order discount is applied per receipt (it distributes linearly, so the sum matches either way). A **fixed** order discount offers "apply to whole bill" and fans out through the existing `distributeFixed()`, remainder onto the largest receipt — the same helper the fixed service charge already uses. No Bill-level money row is invented; storage stays receipt-level.
- **Comp and discount both survive, sharply bounded.** A [[Void (item)|comp]] is order-stage and **removes** the item (reports as a write-off); a 100% line discount is settlement-stage and **keeps the priced line visible** with the reduction beside it (reports as Diskon). Same money, opposite messages to the guest, and a restaurant needs both. Two invariants: a line discount may target only a **non-voided** line, and **no line is counted in both** figures.

## Consequences

- New tables `DiscountPresets` and `Discounts`; `discountAmount` added to `Receipts` and `TableSessionReceipts`. Schema 34 → 35, additive via `_safeAddColumnOn`, no data migration.
- Presets get their **own** endpoint, client repository, WS event, and resync-on-reconnect ([ADR-0021](0021-repository-resync-on-ws-reconnect.md)), mirroring `menu` rather than riding the venue-settings payload. Costs a repository and a DTO for a list that changes monthly; bought consistency with every other synced collection.
- The seed gains a **Kasir** role (`settleBill` + `openDrawer`, deliberately **without** `applyDiscount` — discounts reach the till through step-up unless the owner grants it explicitly). This also fixes a standing contradiction: `CONTEXT.md` described a Kasir role the seed never created, and the seeded Manager lacks `settleBill`, so no seeded non-Admin user could both reach the cashier screen and discount.
- `applyDiscount` was already seeded to Admin and Manager while doing nothing. Wiring it changes those roles' effective powers on upgrade without any role edit — intended, but it is a silent capability activation.
- The Estimasi invariant is weakened, deliberately. `CONTEXT.md` promised the waiter's quote "agrees to the rupiah with what the Cashier later settles"; a discounted bill now settles **lower**. The protection that mattered — one shared `computeBreakdown`, no duplicated rate math — is intact, and a discount is meant to surprise the guest in that direction. The glossary now promises rate-math agreement, not total agreement.
- Deliberately out of scope, each a promo-engine feature with its own trade-offs: validity dates, usage caps, per-item/category targeting, and ad-hoc free-entry discounts. All three slot onto the existing preset row or `Discounts` row later without migrating what this ADR builds.
