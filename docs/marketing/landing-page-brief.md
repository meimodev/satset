# SatSet — Landing Page Brief

> Handoff doc for the website designer. Everything below is product truth translated into marketing language. Feature claims are real and shippable. Use it to write headlines, structure sections, and pick what to show.

---

## One-liner

**The restaurant ordering system that runs on your own Wi-Fi — no internet, no monthly cloud bill, no setup headache.** Install one app, point your phones and tablets at each other, and you're taking orders in minutes.

## The hook (elevator pitch)

SatSet turns ordinary Android phones and tablets into a complete restaurant point-of-order system that lives entirely on your local network. One device becomes the "brain" (the server), the rest become waiter and kitchen stations — all paired in seconds — the waiter and kitchen devices find the server on the network by themselves. Orders, menu, tables, payments, and the kitchen display all sync instantly over your own Wi-Fi.

Because it runs on your LAN, **it keeps working when the internet goes down.** No subscription required to take an order. No cloud round-trip between the waiter's phone and the kitchen.

---

## Who it's for

- Cafés, warungs, restaurants, and bars that want modern table-side ordering without enterprise POS pricing.
- Owners who don't want to depend on a reliable internet connection to run service.
- Teams that need to be up and running the same day they install — no integrator, no wiring, no training week.

## Core promise: every "ease" SatSet delivers

This is the heart of the brief. Each item below is a real, working capability framed as a benefit.

### 1. Easy to set up — minutes, not days
- **One app, two modes.** A single download. Pick "Server" on your main tablet, "Client" on every waiter phone. That's it.
- **Pairing that finds itself.** Devices discover the server automatically on the network and connect with one tap. No IP addresses, no typing, no codes to scan, no manual configuration.
- **Works out of the box.** The menu, tables, and floor plan come pre-seeded so you can explore immediately, then customize.

### 2. Easy to run offline — your network is the whole system
- **Runs on your Wi-Fi, not the cloud.** Orders fly from the waiter's hand to the kitchen over your local network, instantly.
- **Internet outage? Service continues.** Taking orders, firing the kitchen, settling bills — none of it needs the internet.
- **Secure by default.** Traffic between devices is encrypted, and staff sign in with a personal PIN.

### 3. Easy to take orders — built for a busy floor
- **Tap a table, take the order.** Visual floor plan with live table status (open, seated, waiting, ready) so anyone can see the room at a glance.
- **Menu with photos, modifiers, and tags.** Add "no onions," "extra spicy," size options, and custom labels right on the item. Guests get exactly what they asked for.
- **Allergen awareness.** Allergen info stays current and visible, so staff can answer with confidence.
- **Mark items sold out in one tap.** Out of an item? Flip it off the menu instantly — manually or automatically when stock hits zero — and every device updates.
- **Order anywhere, even without a table.** Quick takeaway and menu-first orders for counter service and walk-ups.

### 4. Easy for the kitchen — a live display that keeps up
- **Real-time kitchen display.** Orders appear the instant they're sent. No paper tickets, no shouting.
- **Course firing & order timing.** See how long each order has been waiting, with overdue items flagged automatically.
- **Audio alerts you can tune.** Distinct, selectable sounds for new orders and ready items — pick what cuts through your kitchen's noise.
- **Printer support.** Connect Bluetooth printers for kitchen and receipt printing, with live discovery and connection monitoring.

### 5. Easy to handle real-world floor chaos
- **Move a party to another table** without losing their order — one tap transfers the whole session.
- **Reservations built in.** Take a booking with name, party size, and time; seat them straight from the floor when they arrive.
- **Two waiters, one table, no collisions.** Smart table locking prevents two people editing the same order at once, while still showing who's serving what.
- **Accountable voids.** Cancelling an item is logged to the person who did it — clean accountability without slowing anyone down.

### 6. Easy to get paid — and prove it
- **Split bills and partial settlement.** Settle a table in stages or split across guests.
- **Proof-of-payment capture.** For non-cash payments, snap a photo of the transfer/receipt as proof, attached to the bill.
- **Your branding on the receipt.** Add your venue name and details to receipts so every bill looks like yours.

### 7. Easy to see how business is doing
- **Reports & accounting export.** Pull real, settled sales figures for any date range you choose.
- **Bills, payment proof, and order history in one place.** Everything you need for the books, exportable on demand.
- **Optional cloud snapshots for owners.** Check in on venue performance remotely when you want to — without making the day-to-day depend on the cloud.

### 8. Easy to manage your team
- **Staff accounts with roles.** Give each person exactly the access they need — waiter, kitchen, manager — and nothing they don't.
- **Personal PIN sign-in.** Fast for staff, secure for you. Every action is tied to the person who did it.

## Suggested page structure

1. **Hero** — One-liner + "runs on your Wi-Fi, works offline" + primary CTA (Download / Get Started). Visual: a phone taking an order and a tablet kitchen display, connected.
2. **The 3 big eases** (skimmable): *Set up in minutes* · *Works without internet* · *No subscription to operate.*
3. **How it works** — 3 steps: 1) Install one app, 2) Tap to pair your devices, 3) Start serving. (Lean on how little setup there is.)
4. **Feature grid** — pull from sections 3–8 above, each with an icon + one-line benefit.
5. **Built for the real floor** — moving tables, reservations, split bills, voids, sold-out. The "we thought of the messy parts" section.
6. **Know your numbers** — reports/exports, optional owner cloud snapshots.
8. **Why SatSet** — the differentiator table above.
9. **CTA footer** — download + reassurance ("no internet required to run," "your data stays on your network").

## Tone & voice
- Confident, plain, practical. Restaurant owners are busy — lead with benefits, not tech.
- Emphasize **independence** (your network, your data, no monthly lock-in) and **speed** (set up fast, orders move fast).
- Trust signals to weave in: encrypted device-to-device traffic, PIN-secured staff sign-in, per-action accountability, payment proof capture.

## Key visuals to request/produce
- Floor plan / table status screen (shows the "at a glance" promise).
- Order-taking screen with item photos + modifiers.
- Kitchen display with live timers.
- The pairing moment: a client device finding the server on its own (the magic of setup).

## Words to keep / avoid
- **Keep:** "runs on your Wi-Fi," "works offline," "set up in minutes," "no subscription to operate," "tap to pair."
- **Avoid:** restaurant jargon like "86'd" (use "sold out"), and overpromising cloud features — the cloud parts are *optional extras*, not the foundation.

---

*Platform note for the designer: SatSet is an Android app (phones + tablets). The landing page should drive Android downloads and explain the device setup, not position it as a web SaaS.*
